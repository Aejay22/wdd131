Site Name

The name of the site is SCIENCE FOR KIDS

I chose this name because i know how hard it was for me to understand and comprehend basic science.

Up and coming kids don't have to have the same fate as i did.

Site Purpose

The Purpose of the site is:

  • to build the binds of young children in the subject basic science.
  • to test young children on their understand of the subject and topic(s)
  • so teachers and parents can use it as a guide for their ward(s)

Scenarios

  • What is technology?
  • What is lagos state scheme of work for basic science this term?
  • Do I fully understand what I have been taught in school this week?
